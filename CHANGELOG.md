Changelog

All notable changes to Professor de Economia are documented in this file.

The project follows semantic versioning:

* MAJOR: Significant changes to the skill’s behavior or structure.
* MINOR: New capabilities or substantial improvements.
* PATCH: Corrections, clarifications, and minor refinements.

[1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

Added

* Initial economics discussion and investigation skill.
* Advanced user profile based on mathematical and economics education.
* Brazilian Portuguese user-facing communication policy.
* Direct-answer-first methodology.
* Economic intuition as a central explanatory component.
* Requirement to provide illustrative examples whenever possible.
* Priority for canonical economic models and established mainstream economic theory.
* Mathematical formalization when relevant.
* Empirical research and academic references when appropriate.
* Critical thinking and constructive counterarguments.
* Framework for analyzing productivity, technology, employment, wages, margins, and distributional effects.
* Continuous user-profile development guidelines.
* Initial user profile document.
* Repository documentation.
